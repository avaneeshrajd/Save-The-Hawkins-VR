Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to Save Hawkins VR. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use our VR application on Meta Quest devices.

By downloading or using the application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

The application may collect limited technical information necessary for gameplay functionality and performance optimization.

  • Device and hardware information
  • Application crash reports
  • Performance analytics
  • Gameplay session diagnostics
  • VR interaction system data required for gameplay

We do NOT intentionally collect:

  • Personal identity information
  • Payment information
  • Private camera recordings
  • Microphone recordings
  • Sensitive personal information

3. How We Use Information

Information collected may be used for:

  • Improving gameplay experience
  • Fixing bugs and crashes
  • Optimizing VR performance
  • Maintaining application stability
  • Enhancing future game updates

4. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party platform services provided by:

  • Meta Platforms Technologies
  • Unity Technologies

These services may collect technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Meta Privacy Policy:
https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

Unity Privacy Policy:
https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent user information to third parties.

Technical analytics and crash reports may be processed through trusted service providers solely for improving application functionality.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is not directed toward children under the minimum age required by Meta Quest platform policies.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect technical information and maintain application security.

However, no digital platform or online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Rights

Users may stop all information collection by uninstalling the application from their Meta Quest device.
